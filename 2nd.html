Tyrannosaurus Rex

About Trex

T-rex,was a large meat-eating dinosaur.

It lived about 66 to 68 million years ago.

It had powerful jaws,sharp teeth and strong legs

What did T-rex eat

T-rex was carnivore.

It ate meat and may have hunted other animals or eaten animals that were already dead

Interesting Facts

  • T-rex walked on 2 legs
  • It had very powerful jaws
  • It had sharp teeth
  • It lived during the Late cretaceous period.
